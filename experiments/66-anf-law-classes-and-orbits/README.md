# Experiment 66 — ANF law classes and orbits

The complete ANF coefficient vectors are quotiented by identical violation functions.

```text
component  distinct ANF laws  XOR closure  defect-state orbits  predicate orbits
8                    3           no                3                  2
11                  15           no               10                  9
```

Every law contains the universal full-degree monomial. Lower-order ANF masks, law multiplicities, exact XOR-closure tests, and orbit partitions under the verified reversal action are preserved.

The counts `3 = 2²−1` and `15 = 2⁴−1` do not imply a vector-space structure for the raw violation-law families: those families are not XOR-closed linear subspaces. Affine or translated-linear structure after factoring out the universal full-degree term is left open for Experiment 67. These are exact finite representation results, not semantic-complexity or storage-cost claims.
